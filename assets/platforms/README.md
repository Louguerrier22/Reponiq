# Platform badges

These marks identify repository hosts inside Reponiq. They are secondary to Reponiq's own identity, retain their original geometry/colors and do not imply endorsement. SVGs are bundled locally, rendered as images, and make no network requests.

| Asset | Source and terms checked 2026-11-05 |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub | Official [favicon](https://github.githubassets.com/favicons/favicon.svg). [GitHub brand guidelines](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo) permit integration identification. The original dark mark sits on a white circular background. |
| Bitbucket | Official [logo](https://dam-cdn.atl.orangelogic.com/AssetLink/402m3wfwyr4y2p46r38jh80ycj55ub8f.svg), linked by [Atlassian's trademark guidelines](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/trademark). Used without modification to identify compatibility. |
| Gitea | [Gitea source logo](https://github.com/go-gitea/gitea/blob/main/assets/logo.svg), distributed under its [MIT license](GITEA-LICENSE.txt). Copyright 2016 The Gitea Authors; 2015 The Gogs Authors. |
| Codeberg | [Simple Icons vector](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/codeberg.svg), [CC0](CC0-LICENSE.txt). Codeberg's own [documentation](https://docs.codeberg.org/improving-codeberg/merch/) states that its logos have a public-domain dedication. |

GitLab is identified by its name and a neutral Git icon. Its [trademark guidelines](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/marketing/brand-experience/trademark-guidelines/) do not permit third-party product-interface logo use without separate permission. Azure DevOps, Forgejo, SourceHut and unidentified hosts also use neutral icons with platform names; no permission for their branded artwork is asserted.

Names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. This asset review is scoped evidence, not a legal certification. Recheck brand rules before changing the use or publishing a new package.
